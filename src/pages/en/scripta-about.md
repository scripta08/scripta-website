*SCRIPTA*, the official journal of the Hunmin jeongeum Society, is a refereed journal published annually in October. *SCRIPTA* welcomes submissions from scholars of various disciplines whose work especially pertains to writing systems. Submissions should not substantially duplicate work that any of the authors have published elsewhere.
